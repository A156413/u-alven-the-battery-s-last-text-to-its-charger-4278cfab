Entrants write the phone battery's last text message to the charger it waited for all day, as one line of plain text of at most 200 characters. A judge model reads the lines two at a time and keeps the funnier one, round after round, with the board blind until results are published. The best-ranked lines take the top 8 places of the pool.

## The entry

> 1%. i waited all day by the outlet while you charged his phone, so i'm breaking up with you by text. fitting, honestly. tell the toaster i said hi — she always shows up.

169 characters (limit 200). The battery breaks up with its charger by text — fitting, since it *is* a text — and the punchline lands in the final words: the toaster, the appliance that always shows up.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). Deterministic: no network, no randomness, no clock.
